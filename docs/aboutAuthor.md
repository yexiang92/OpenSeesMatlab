# About Me

I'm [Yexiang Yan (闫业祥)](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1434-6710), a
postdoctoral researcher at McGill University in Canada, where I work with
[Prof. Yazhou Xie](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fVIZH78AAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).

My research sits at the intersection of computational mechanics, structural
reliability, and data-driven engineering. I am particularly interested in the
resilience and seismic performance of civil infrastructure, as well as how
structures deteriorate over their service lives.

I received my Ph.D. in Bridge Engineering from *Tongji University* and my M.S.
and B.S. degrees in Civil Engineering from *Southwest Jiaotong University*.

My current interests include:

- seismic fragility and lifecycle risk assessment
- uncertainty quantification
- structural deterioration and aging
- multi-hazard performance assessment
- automated nonlinear modeling, analysis, and post-processing

I also develop computational tools that make structural modeling, nonlinear
analysis, and post-processing easier to automate. This work often combines
physics-based simulation with surrogate models and machine learning.

My work has appeared in *Reliability Engineering & System Safety*, *Engineering
Structures*, *Soil Dynamics and Earthquake Engineering*, *Structures*, and
*SoftwareX*.
